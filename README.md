# Website Speed Optimization Guide for Small Businesses in India

A slow website loses customers before they ever see what you sell. In India, many visitors browse on mobile phones over 4G or patchy connections, so even a one-second delay can reduce enquiries, sales and search rankings. This guide explains practical, low-cost steps any small business can take to make its website load faster.

## 1. Compress and resize images

Images are usually the largest files on a page. Resize photos to the size they are actually displayed, compress them with tools such as TinyPNG or Squoosh, and use modern formats like WebP. A product photo rarely needs to be wider than 1200 pixels, and a banner can often be under 200 KB without visible loss of quality.

## 2. Use caching and a CDN

Browser caching lets returning visitors reuse files instead of downloading them again. A content delivery network (CDN) serves your pages from servers closer to the visitor, which matters for customers across India. Enable caching in your hosting control panel or through a plugin, and set sensible expiry times for images, stylesheets and scripts.

## 3. Choose fast, reliable hosting

Shared hosting on an overloaded server will slow even a well-built site. Choose a host with servers in or near India, solid-state storage and support for the latest PHP or server software. Keep your content management system, theme and plugins updated, and remove plugins you no longer use.

## 4. Measure Core Web Vitals

Google's Core Web Vitals measure loading speed, interactivity and visual stability. Test your site with PageSpeed Insights, aim for a Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 seconds, and fix the largest problems first — usually heavy images, slow server response or render-blocking scripts.

## 5. Design mobile-first

Most Indian customers will visit your site on a phone first. Design for small screens, use a responsive layout, keep menus simple, avoid pop-ups that cover the screen, and test on a real phone over mobile data, not only on office Wi-Fi.

Small, regular improvements add up. Start with images and hosting, measure the result, then work through the list again.
